Tools

Tactics

Reads your team's tactic to show who fits best in every spot and highlight empty or weak spots. You can also edit a tactic and export it as a new tactic file without changing anything live in the game.

#What the Tactics screen does

Click Tactics under Tools in the left sidebar to open it. When you open it with a save loaded in FM27, FMAX reads your manager's club tactics and first-team squad and lays everything out on a single screen.

  • Use it just to check your lineup. It suggests a starter and candidate for every spot, and flags any empty or weak spots.
  • Edit and experiment. Try out different roles, Player Instructions, Team Instructions, and Mentality right here, then export your version as a new tactic file.
  • Nothing inside the running game is changed. Your edits only enter the game when you load the exported file on FM27's tactics screen.
Good to know

Even when FM27 isn't running, you can still open saved tactic files. Because FMAX can't read your squad offline, starters and candidates stay blank.

#Around the screen

#Choosing a tactic

Pick what to view from the Tactic row at the top of the screen.

  • In-game tactics: Shows buttons for each tactic currently set up on your tactics screen in FM27. The one your team is using right now is marked 'In use'.
  • Open saved tactic file: Opens files from your game's tactics folder (Sports Interactive\Football Manager 27\tactics inside your Documents folder). Downloaded tactics placed in that folder show up here too, or you can drag and drop a tactic file (.fmf) straight into the FMAX window without moving it first. Below that, Tactics folder shows which folder FMAX is reading right now, and Choose folder lets you point it somewhere else.

Click Reload in the top-right corner to re-read your current tactics and squad from the game—handy right after you tweak your tactic or lineup in FM27.

#The pitch

Your tactic's positions and roles are laid out on the pitch (Attacks toward top).

  • Use Combined / In Possession / Out of Possession above the pitch to choose which phase's shape and roles to view. Combined shows the In Possession positions with both phases' roles listed together.
  • Under each spot's role, FMAX shows the name of its suggested starter.
  • Empty spot: No one in your current first-team squad fits that spot as a starter.
  • Weak spot: That spot's starter is weaker than your team average.

#Spot details

Click any spot on the pitch to inspect it on the right. Click the spot again or click Clear selection to close the panel.

RowMeaning
In-game pickThe player currently assigned to that spot on the game's tactics screen. Marked 'Differs from suggestion' if it doesn't match FMAX's pick
Suggested starterThe player in your current first team who FMAX rates as the best fit for this spot
CandidateThe next-best fit for this spot behind the starter
In Possession / Out of PossessionThe Position, Roles, Duty, and any custom Player Instructions set for each phase

Next to each player's name you'll see their ability for this spot, age, condition, Match sharpness, and Role ability. Hover over any unfamiliar term to see what it means, or click a player's name to open their edit tab.

When no spot is selected, the right panel shows Spots to fix first (starting with empty or weak spots), Picked in game (how many of your in-game starters differ from FMAX's suggestions), and Left out due to injury.

Starters and candidates are chosen by the same rules as Recruitment analysis: among first-team players familiar enough with the position, FMAX picks in order of highest ability for that spot, puts each player in at most one spot, and leaves out injured players.

#Team Instructions

Instructions for the whole team are grouped by phase. Dimmed text means the instruction is still at its default setting.

#Editing a tactic

Click Edit tactic in the top-right corner to enter edit mode. A bar at the top shows 'Editing' along with how many changes you've made.

  • Name and Mentality: Change these above the pitch.
  • Position, Roles, Duty, and Player Instructions: Click a spot on the pitch and pick them on the right. You set In Possession and Out of Possession separately. Instructions marked 'Locked by role' are built into that role and can't be changed.
  • Team Instructions: Pick these in the Team Instructions area below the pitch.
ButtonWhat it does
Revert changesDiscards every edit made here and goes back to the original tactic
Done editingCloses edit mode. Your edits stay in memory so you can reopen edit mode and keep working
Export to fileSaves your edited tactic to the game's tactics folder as a new file, leaving the original tactic and file untouched

After exporting, head to the tactics screen in FM27 and load the new file to use it.

Good to know

Nothing changes in your live game while you edit here. Load the exported file inside FM27 to put it into play.

Good to know:

  • The goalkeeper spot can't be moved, and you can't drag a spot onto one that's already occupied.
  • Roles and Player Instructions that FM27 doesn't allow for that position and formation can't be selected.
  • Files saved in an older game format are view-only and can't be edited. Load the tactic in FM27 and save it again first.
  • You can't export if your game's tactics folder has no saved tactic files in it at all. Save any tactic once inside FM27 first.

#See which works best in the Tactic Arena

Located at the bottom of edit mode, this tool creates variants of your current tactic that change just one thing and adds them to the Tactic Arena test queue. Because the Arena runs every variant under identical conditions, you can see cleanly how much that one tweak moves your points.

  1. Choose What to vary: Mentality, a single Team Instruction, or the role of a selected spot. To compare roles, click a spot on the pitch first.
  2. Click Queue variants in Arena. FMAX builds one tactic file for each alternative of the setting you picked and adds them straight to the Tactic Arena's test queue without writing files to your game's tactics folder.
  3. Start the test from the Run tests tab in the Tactic Arena.

#Ask Eri

Clicking Ask Eri in the top-right corner opens the Eri chat panel with a pre-filled question about this tactic. Eri knows which tactic you're viewing and whether a spot is selected: if you've picked a spot, the prompt asks who fits best there; if no spot is selected, it asks about weak spots and clashing instructions.
