Edit tabs

Team

View and edit an individual squad within a club—such as the First Team, Reserves, B Team, U21s, or U18s. Change team reputation, and use the ⚡ menu to handle injuries, bans, unhappiness, and morale across the whole squad.

#How to open

  • Click a name in the Teams list.
  • Click a squad table heading (such as First Team) on a Club tab's Squad page.
  • Click a team in the Teams list on a Club tab's Info page.
  • Click a team anywhere teams appear, such as a player's Competition Registration list.

If the team has its own dedicated name, that name is shown; otherwise it uses the club's name. Teams belonging to women's clubs appear in the women's team color.

#Tab header

  • Below the name, you'll see the Unique ID, Club, Team type, and League. Click any underlined name to open its tab.
  • There is a single number card: Reputation (0–10000). Click it to type a number directly.
  • The buttons on the right are ⚡ In-game editor actions, Presets, Locks, Bookmarks, Compare, and Reload from game. They work the same way as on the Player tab header.

Goes into the game when you press Save

Changes to Reputation only take effect in the game once you click Save on the bottom bar (or Save Ctrl+S at the top).

The 'Manager' and 'Captain' columns from the Teams list appear only in the list view, not on this tab.

#⚡ menu

The Team tab's ⚡ menu has two groups:

  • All players: Remove all player injuries, Remove all player bans, Remove all player unhappiness, Change team morale
  • Team: Improve team cohesion, Max tactical familiarity
Written to the game at once

Actions in the ⚡ menu apply to the game immediately when you click 'Apply now' in the confirmation dialog, and cannot be undone.

See Game editor (⚡) for what each item does.
