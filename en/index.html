Getting started

Using FMAX

FMAX lets you edit player, club, and competition values live while FM27 is running. This wiki covers everything from initial setup to every screen in the app.

Good to know

FMAX was previously called FMLE. If you were already using FMLE, just update—it switches over to FMAX automatically and keeps all your settings and history.

#New to FMAX?

If this is your first time using FMAX, follow the two pages below in order. You'll go from installing the app to saving your first edit in about 10 minutes.
